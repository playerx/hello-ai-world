Useit.design — Workspace for UI/UX designers

Useit makes it easier for designers to manage and streamline their work.

Role
UX Researcher & Co-founder
Industry
SaaS
Duration
6 months

Project overview

Useit.design is a productivity platform built specifically for UX/UI professionals — both design teams and solo designers. It centralizes research, tasks, and collaboration into one place. AI features enhance the workflow by summarizing insights, suggesting next steps, and tracking progress.

Problem

Most project management tools aren't built for UX/UI designers. Teams lose time jumping between different apps, which causes scattered notes, repeated work, and weak collaboration. Many task tools also include fields that designers don't really need, making the process slower instead of easier.

Solution

Together with my team, we built Useit — the first Figma-integrated task management tool made for UX/UI designers. The platform:

  • Reduced task creation from 15 steps to just 2
  • Centralized research documentation and insights
  • Integrated smoothly with existing design tools
  • Delivered an intuitive interface designers enjoy using
  • Introduced AI-powered UX consultancy to guide workflows

Stage 1 · Empathize

As UX/UI designers ourselves, our first step was to validate the problem: were others facing the same challenges? We conducted 30+ interviews with designers across industries, experience levels, and locations. While our primary focus was the U.S. market, we also included global voices to gain a broader perspective.

Stage 2 · Define

Interviews revealed key pain points and new feature ideas. A competitive analysis showed where generic tools fall short, giving us a strong foundation for ideation. During this stage, we were selected by one of the largest accelerator programs — over two months we learned from UC Berkeley faculty and Silicon Valley experts, expanded our investor network, and secured our first investment.

Stage 3 · Ideation

One of our biggest strengths was doing everything in-house — research, design, and development — allowing us to move fast. We built a landing page, a beta version, and quickly moved to an MVP.

Stage 4 · Prototype & test

For testing, we observed user sign-ups, assigned tasks, and asked follow-up questions. We conducted 10 usability tests with the beta version and collected feedback from over 40 friends & family customers. This revealed usability issues, bugs, and new opportunities to improve the product.

Stage 5 · Implementation & launch support

After testing, we prioritized and implemented the most in-demand features for our AI agent. Since launch, we've been actively supporting users, improving the product based on feedback, and continuously training the AI to provide personalized UX consultancy tailored to each user's needs.

Outcomes

Through a structured design thinking process, we transformed a broad problem into a validated product with real traction. Today, the product continues to evolve and grow.
