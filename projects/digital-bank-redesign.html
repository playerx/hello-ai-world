Redesign project: Home screen of digital bank

In 2022, I joined a fintech company that manages two digital banking applications. By 2024, our team took on the challenge of redesigning the home page — the app's most visited screen and the starting point for nearly every user journey.

Role
UX Researcher & Designer
Industry
Fintech
Duration
3 months

Research goal

Understand how users interact with the home page:

  • Which elements capture their attention?
  • Where do they begin navigation?
  • What are their primary needs when opening the app?
  • How does our experience compare to other digital banks in the region?

Methodology

  • Analytics tools: Posthog & UXCam
  • Competitor analysis: 8 regional banking apps
  • Feedback sources: Monthly NPS & customer service reports

Process

  1. Analytics (Posthog): Measured the most and least clickable events on the home screen, tracking both total clicks and unique users.
  2. Behavioral insights (UXCam): Analyzed heatmaps and reviewed 50+ user session recordings to uncover friction points.
  3. Journey mapping: Traced user flows from app entry to task completion to identify where users dropped off.
  4. Feedback loop: Cross-referenced analytics with monthly NPS and customer service data to ensure design decisions aligned with user needs.

Key insights & decisions

  • 3 main action buttons: Analytics confirmed they were essential. We kept their placement to maintain consistency.
  • Bottom navigation bar: Previously missing. Adding it aligned us with 8 competitors and gave users faster access to core features.
  • Support: Previously hidden; relocated to the top of the screen for visibility.
  • Notifications: Moved out of the avatar (where they were often ignored) and displayed as a separate icon at the top.

Validation before development

To ensure our redesign truly matched user expectations, we conducted usability testing with 8 participants. This step allowed us to gather feedback on clarity, navigation, and feature visibility before moving into development.

Reflections

This project showed how combining analytics, user feedback, and testing leads to clear design improvements. The redesign made navigation faster, support easier to find, and key actions more visible — all directly shaped by user needs.
