UAE agency collaborations

Alongside my full-time roles, I've collaborated as a freelance designer with international clients. While working with a UAE-based digital marketing agency, I redesigned a range of landing pages and e-commerce sites across different industries:

  • Coffee Company Landing Page — redesigned with warm, inviting visuals to reflect the brand's culture and product quality.
  • Arabic Perfume Landing Page — given a more elegant and luxurious design that balances tradition with modern appeal.
  • Furniture Store Website — refreshed with clean layouts and improved usability to support product discovery and sales.

Direct client work

Beyond the UAE projects, I also collaborated directly with clients on:

  • Canadian Property Investment Website — designed to build trust and professionalism for international investors.
  • Fine Jewelry E-commerce — a concept project where I designed a refined, detail-oriented online store to highlight exclusivity.

Reflections

Each project reflects my ability to adapt design language to the brand, market, and audience — balancing cultural elements with modern design principles.
